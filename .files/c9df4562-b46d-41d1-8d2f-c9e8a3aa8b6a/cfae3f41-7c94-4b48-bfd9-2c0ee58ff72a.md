## 1. Executive Summary

The project aims to deliver an AI‑driven coaching platform that standardizes onboarding training, provides real‑time competency insights, and automates reporting for retail associates. Scope includes a web portal, mobile app, AI coach engine, document ingestion with OCR, analytics dashboards, and integration with existing LMS and Microsoft SSO. The proposed hybrid cloud SaaS architecture leverages Azure services to meet strict audit, retention, and compliance requirements while supporting mobile‑first adoption. Recommendation: proceed with the Azure‑centric serverless solution, focusing on an MVP that validates core coaching, authentication, and reporting capabilities before scaling.

## 9. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Azure OpenAI token and Cognitive Search vector query rate limits could be exceeded during bulk document ingestion and high‑frequency coaching sessions. | Service throttling would cause delays in AI responses and ingestion failures, breaking the real‑time coaching and weekly reporting pipelines. | Implement a staged ingestion queue with Azure Service Bus, batch OCR and indexing, and configure APIM throttling policies plus exponential back‑off retries. monitor usage dashboards and negotiate higher quota with Microsoft early in the project. |
| OCR inaccuracies and duplicate/poorly named PDFs may produce low‑quality searchable text, leading to irrelevant AI context and hallucinated coaching advice. | Incorrect or missed document fragments will reduce coaching relevance, increase manual correction effort, and jeopardize compliance (unaudited content). | Create a pre‑ingest metadata pipeline that de‑duplicates files using hash comparison, enforces a naming convention, and validates OCR confidence scores. Flag low‑confidence pages for manual review before indexing. |
| Fallback from voice to text in low‑bandwidth stores may not trigger reliably, causing a poor user experience and abandonment of the mobile app. | Associates in locations with spotty connectivity could lose access to coaching, defeating the adoption goal. | Integrate Azure Speech Service with network quality detection on the client side; if latency > threshold, automatically switch UI to text‑only mode and cache audio locally for later upload. Conduct field testing in representative stores before full rollout. |

## 11. Architecture Summary

The hybrid cloud SaaS design leverages Azure's serverless and AI services to meet strict audit, retention, and SSO mandates while keeping operational costs low. By separating the compliance layer (immutable Blob storage and Cosmos DB audit logs) from the scalable coaching engine (Azure Functions, OpenAI, Cognitive Search), the architecture delivers fast, secure, and globally performant experiences for both web and mobile users. This alignment ensures consistent training, reliable analytics, and future‑proof extensibility within the client’s budget and governance constraints.
